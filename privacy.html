Money Talk Manager: privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Money Talk Manager is a private tool used only by the owner of the Money Talk Animations channels (YouTube and Instagram) to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Google / YouTube: the owner's channel details, uploading the channel's own videos, thumbnails and playlists, reading comments on those videos, and the channel's own analytics (views, watch time).
  • Instagram: the owner's account details, publishing the account's own Reels, reading comments, and the account's own insights.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos, to show the owner how they perform, and to plan new videos.
  • Comments and performance data may be processed by AI model providers used by the tool (for example, to summarise viewer questions and suggestions into topic ideas). Only the text needed for that task is sent; commenters' names and profile details are not. The data is not used to train AI models.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is never sold, shared with anyone other than the AI model providers described above, or used for advertising.
  • Raw comments and statistics are kept only as long as needed to produce the ideas and reports, and are refreshed or deleted in line with the platforms' policies.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Local copies are deleted by the owner.
